Farmer Guide & Cold Storage Tips

Simple guidelines for optimal vegetable preservation and shelf life.

🥬 🥦 🥕

Zone 2: 0°C to 2°C (Center)

Best for: Cabbage, Cauliflower, Carrots, Spinach.
Tip: Keep crates in the center chamber. These crops require near freezing temperatures and 95%+ humidity.

🫘 🫑 🌿

Zone 1: 2°C to 8°C

Best for: French Beans, Capsicum / Bell Peppers.
Tip: Do not freeze beans below 2°C to avoid russet browning. Zone 1 provides optimal gentle cool air.

🍅 🌶 🥔 🫚

Zone 3: 8°C to 15°C

Best for: Tomatoes, Green Chilli, Potatoes, Ginger, Bhut Jolokia.
Tip: Never store tomatoes or tropical ginger at 0°C to avoid chilling decay. Store in Zone 3.
