Farmer Guide & Cold Storage Tips

Simple, easy-to-follow rules to get the best shelf life and market prices for your vegetables.

🥬 🥦 🥕

Zone 2: 0°C to 2°C (Center)

Best for: Cabbage, Cauliflower, Carrots, Spinach, Mustard Greens.
Tip: Keep crates near center for maximum cooling. These vegetables love near freezing temperatures and 95%+ humidity.

🫘 🫑 🌿

Zone 1: 2°C to 8°C

Best for: French Beans, Capsicum / Bell Peppers, Tender Pods.
Tip: Do not freeze beans below 2°C or they will turn brown. Zone 1 provides gentle cool air.

🍅 🌶 🥔 🫚

Zone 3: 8°C to 15°C

Best for: Tomatoes, Green Chilli, Potatoes, Ginger, Bhut Jolokia.
Tip: Never put tomatoes or ginger in 0°C (they will spoil with chilling injury). Keep them in Zone 3!

Frequently Asked Questions

❓ When should I use 1-Zone Mode instead of 3-Zone Mode?

Use 1-Zone Mode when you have a bumper harvest of a single crop (e.g. 500 kg of cabbage) and you want the entire cold storage room to maintain one uniform temperature.

❓ What should I do if a "High Temperature" warning appears?

1. Check that the door is firmly closed and the magnetic seal is airtight.
2. Verify that the solar panel breakers are turned on.
3. Make sure crates are not blocking the cold air vents from the center chamber.

❓ How does the UV-C and TiO₂ system help?

The UV-C light runs automatically for 15 minutes to kill airborne bacteria and mould spores. Combined with the titanium dioxide (TiO₂) filter, it keeps the storage air clean and neutralizes spoilage gases.
